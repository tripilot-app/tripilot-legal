Delete your data – Tripilot

Tripilot · last updated: October 20, 2026

Version française

Tripilot has no account: your trips are stored on your phone, and that is where you delete them.

1. In the app

  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase all data. Deletes your trips, activities, places, expenses, documents, photos and conversations with the assistant.
  • One conversation with the assistant: in the assistant, ⋮ menu → Clear the conversation.
  • One trip: deleting it also deletes its conversation with the assistant.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored on the phone.

2. What exists outside the phone, and what happens to it

Only if you used the travel assistant:

  • Message counter (Tripilot relay): a number of messages, filed under a fingerprint of your purchase receipt, without your name or your conversation. Pro's monthly counters are deleted automatically about two months later; a trip pass's counter is kept while the pass is valid.
  • Messages sent to the assistant (Anthropic): kept by Anthropic for a limited time under its terms, then deleted automatically. They are not used to train its models.
  • Reported answers (Tripilot relay): if you reported one of the assistant's answers, the answer, your question and the reason are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically. They are tied to nothing that identifies you: to have one deleted sooner, give its date and content.
  • Technical logs (Cloudflare): no message content, kept for a few days.

Your purchases themselves are handled by Google Play; for those, contact Google.

3. Make a request

For any deletion request or question, write to tripilotsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Tripilot". Since the developer holds neither your name nor your trips, say what you would like deleted; you will get an answer within 30 days.
