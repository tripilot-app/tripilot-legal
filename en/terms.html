The app

Tripilot helps you plan and follow your trips. Basic use is free. By installing or using the app, you accept these terms.

Tripilot Pro

Tripilot Pro unlocks extra features (unlimited trips, itinerary generation, export, multi-currency expenses). It comes in several forms, each priced in the app before any purchase:

  • Monthly or yearly subscription: renews automatically at the end of each period, at the price then in effect, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. You can cancel anytime in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). Pro stays active until the end of the period already paid. If a free trial is offered, it turns into a paid subscription when it ends unless cancelled before.
  • Lifetime: a one-time payment, no subscription.
  • Trip pass: a one-time payment that unlocks Pro for a single trip, the one it was bought from. It cannot be moved to another trip, and it is gone if that trip is deleted.

Purchases are tied to your Google Play account. On a new device, "Restore purchases" reactivates a subscription or a lifetime purchase. A trip pass is stored with its trip, on the device.
