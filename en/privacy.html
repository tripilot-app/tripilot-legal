Version française

In short

Tripilot is a travel planner that works on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no tracker and no analytics. Your trips, expenses, documents and photos stay on your device. The developer has no access to any of them.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (trips, activities, places, expenses, notes, imported documents and photos) is stored in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent to a server run by the developer: there is none.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google Drive, encrypted, as it does for every app. Imported documents and photos are excluded and never leave the device that way. You can turn this backup off in Android settings.

Export ("Export my trips") creates an archive that you choose where to save or share.

What is sent to third-party services

For some features the app queries online services directly. They receive the request and, like any web server, your IP address. No personal identifier, name or email address is sent.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your card, name or email address. The app only receives from Google Play the list of active purchases, to unlock Pro. For a trip pass, an internal trip number is attached to the purchase so it can be activated on the right trip; that number carries no information about the trip itself. Payment data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push notification service is used.

Children

Tripilot is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data about them.

Your rights

Since the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on their side. To erase everything, use "Erase all data" in Settings, or uninstall the app. For purchases, contact Google Play.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
